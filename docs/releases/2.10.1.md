# Bazis 2.10.1

## Fixes

- **The errors are in the language of the request.** The title of `ERR_ITEM_INVALID`
  stayed `Validation error` under a translated detail: the ru catalog of the core
  translated the titles of the errors (`Validation error`, `Request error`,
  `Permission denied: ...`) as themselves and lacked `Invalid filter`, and the details of
  the routes were plain strings. The titles and the fixed details the core answers with
  are lazy and translated into Russian now: `Item not found` (404), the read-only
  relationship (`ERR_RELATIONSHIP_READONLY`), the relation access (`ERR_RELATION_ACCESS`),
  `Data type is invalid`, `Data conflict`, an included object that does not exist (its
  detail, which had a typo, is `The object does not exist. Type: <type>. ID: <id>`) and
  the protected deletion (`MODEL_NOT_JSONAPI`, `MODEL_PROTECTED_RELATION`). The English
  texts are unchanged otherwise. The Pydantic messages of `ERR_VALIDATE` and the
  diagnostics of `ERR_FILTER` stay English.

## Changes

- **A JSON:API HTTP error raised in a route keeps its code.** `route_run` rebuilt every
  `HTTPException` as a `JsonApiHttpException` without its code: a `JsonApi403Exception`
  of a route or a hook (bazis-permit, bazis-users) answered `ERR_REQUEST` instead of
  `ERR_FORBIDDEN` (`JsonApi401Exception`: `ERR_UNAUTHORIZED`, a custom `code` was lost
  too). A `JsonApiHttpException` now passes through unchanged; a plain `HTTPException`
  of a route is still `ERR_REQUEST`. A client that matched `ERR_REQUEST` on a 403 reads
  `ERR_FORBIDDEN`.

- **`route_search_fields(route_cls)`** (`bazis.core.utils.query_complex`): the search
  fields a route searches, as declared (with their lookup prefix), those its LIST schema
  reaches; `bazis.W007` reports the others with it. A tool can use it instead of
  repeating the check (bazis-front can switch to it; it has not yet).

## Documentation

The guide (`bazis/core/AGENTS.md`) and the manifest answer what the agents of a trial read
the sources for: the hooks of the writes of a route and their order (what
`hook_before_update` sees, where `validate_item` runs), the errors (the pointer of a
`JsonApiBazisError` from its `loc`, the status of a `JsonApiBazisException`, what the
application answers for each exception, that a Django `ValidationError` outside
`validate_item` is a 500 and that `full_clean()` is not called), `SchemaFields`
(`origin`, `include`, `exclude`, `is_inherit`), a second route set of a model
(`get_url_prefix`, `default_route`) and a read-only route (`actions`, the names of the
write actions, `action_dict_data`).

## Erratum to 2.10.0

- **2.10.0 changed no pointer of an existing error; the errors of the core point in two
  ways.** The errors of `validate_item` (`ERR_ITEM_INVALID`), like those of the relation
  access (2.7), point into the document: `/data/attributes/<field>`,
  `/data/relationships/<field>`, `/data`; that is the contract. The errors of the request
  schemas (`ERR_VALIDATE`) still point to `/attributes/<field>` and
  `/relationships/<field>`, also for an included item: a known defect (the prefix was
  lost in `model_validate`), fixed in 2.11.0. A `JsonApiBazisError` points where its
  `loc` says:
  `loc=('attributes', field)` is `/attributes/<field>`. So a rule moved from a hook that
  raised `JsonApiBazisError(loc=('attributes', field))` into `validate_item` moves its
  error from `/attributes/<field>` to `/data/attributes/<field>`. A client that maps the
  errors to the fields of a form must read both; the client of bazis-front reads only
  `/attributes/<field>` up to its release that reads `/data/...`: upgrade it before moving
  the rules of a product into `validate_item`.
- **`validate_item` runs after the hooks of the write.** The routes validate once, at the
  end of the request: `hook_after_create`, `hook_after_update` and
  `hook_after_relationships_change` see an item that is not validated yet. A transit of
  bazis-statusy made in `hook_after_create` runs its validators before the invariants of
  the item, so an invalid item could get the error of the transit (`ERR_TRANSIT`, without
  a pointer) instead of the error of its field. A hook that needs a valid item validates
  the writes so far first: `with defer_validate_item() as scope: scope.validate()`; what is
  written after it is validated again at the end of the request. The order stays: the
  writes of the hooks are validated with the item, once.
